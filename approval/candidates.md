## 1. Baby Elephant Rescued from the River  (animals, impact 9/10)
A deeply emotional and inspiring story has emerged from West Bengal's Jalpaiguri district. A three-to-four-day-old baby elephant was swept away here in the Teesta River. While crossing the river with its mother and the herd, it got separated in the strong current. Five fishermen fishing in the river near Malbazar saw it drowning. Without fear, these fishermen immediately turned their boat toward the baby elephant. They chased it by boat for three kilometers in the river's swift flow. After a lot of effort, the fishermen safely pulled the exhausted calf onto the boat. Inside the boat, the fishermen hugged the baby elephant to support it. Later, veterinary doctor Nick Doley thoroughly examined and cared for it. Forest department officials reunited the baby elephant with its mother. Everyone is wholeheartedly praising the courageous effort of the fishermen.

## 2. Match Disappointment Becomes a Lifesaver  (people, impact 9/10)
Forty-year-old Alastair Cass was watching a match of his favorite football team, Arsenal, on TV. In the match, Arsenal suffered a heavy three-nil defeat against Brighton. Deeply frustrated by this loss, Alastair walked out of his house in anger. As soon as he stepped out, he suddenly heard the cries and screams of a woman. The woman was trapped chest-deep in mud and desperately needed help to save her life. Right after his team's defeat, Alastair stepped forward to save the woman's life and became a true hero for her.

## 3. Homeless Students Get a Safe Home  (education, impact 8/10)
A wonderful initiative has been launched for college students in need. The Dax Program has opened a new hostel for students in Denver, USA. This organization provides housing for up to two years to students facing homelessness or financial hardship. This allows students to focus on completing their degrees without worrying about housing. This historic brick building in Denver has accommodation for fifteen students. The program is working in partnership with Metropolitan State University and Regis University. On this occasion, a student named Nikya Harrell shared her story. In the early days, Nikya had no roof over her head and was extremely distressed. With the help of the Dax Program, she found safe shelter, and her life improved. So far, this organization has provided shelter to nearly five hundred students across different cities.

## 4. Architect Shaping Cities Through Sound  (people, impact 8/10)
In the world of architecture, a blind architect has done inspiring work. Chris Downey had been working as a normal architect for twenty years. In 2008, he lost his sight after a surgery. But Chris never lost heart in the face of this great challenge. He realized that paths and buildings could also be understood through sound. Chris began identifying directions from the echo of the wind chimes outside his home. After this, he incorporated elements of sound and touch into the design of buildings. He is no longer limited to just ramps or Braille. He has also helped design San Francisco's famous Salesforce Transit Center. His goal is to make public spaces accessible for blind and disabled people.

## 5. 500 Lakes to Be Revived in Karnataka  (environment, impact 8/10)
A major plan has been prepared to revive lakes in Karnataka. The state government has drawn up a 150-crore rupee plan to rejuvenate five hundred lakes across seven water-scarce districts. This project will be completed with assistance from the World Bank. These five hundred lakes include sixty-eight in Bengaluru Rural, sixty-seven in Bengaluru Urban, ninety in Chikkaballapur, ninety-six in Tumakuru, eighty-six in Kolar, forty-nine in Kalaburagi, and forty-four in Bidar. Under this project, silt will be removed from the lakes, embankments will be raised and strengthened, canals will be repaired, and beautification will be carried out. According to the World Bank, this network could help manage floods and droughts.

## 6. Campaign to Save Rare Apples  (environment, impact 7/10)
Dave Benscoter is a retired FBI agent. For more than twenty years, he has been searching for century-old apple trees to save them from extinction. An incident related to this unique work took place in the year 2012. At that time, Dave was pruning trees in the backyard of a friend's house. While working there, he found an apple he had never seen before. Ever since, he has been continuously working to discover and preserve such century-old trees.

## 7. Elders' Experience Outweighs AI  (people, impact 7/10)
According to a new survey conducted in the UK, more than seven out of ten people believe that AI chatbots can never replace the advice of elders. The study of two thousand adults found that twenty-one percent of people use these artificial intelligence tools for help several times a week. Despite this, ninety-two percent of people believe that certain forms of wisdom and knowledge can only be gained through life experience. Seventy-one percent of participants in the study feel that AI can never take the place of lessons taught by elders.
